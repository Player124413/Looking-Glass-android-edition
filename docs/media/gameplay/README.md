The MP4 files use H.264, 1280 × 720, 30 frames per second, at normal speed.
Each is one continuous excerpt with only its beginning and end trimmed.
The looping GIFs show three seconds from those same recordings, reduced to
360 pixels wide and 8 frames per second for the README. No desktop, menus or
personal save data are included. Subtitles were disabled.

**These clips are silent.** The isolated recording session did not provide
an audio output. This is a capture limitation, not a statement about game audio.

## Edited preview showcase

not rendering performance at 4K. Original artwork and this publicity video are

## Story and opening-chapter videos

Recorded and edited on 1 October 2026 from the same released executable above.
The [78-second story showcase](https://github.com/skulitom/LookingGlass/releases/download/v0.32.0-preview.1/LookingGlass-Story-Showcase.mp4) adds footage from **Dementia** (`gvillage`)
and **Pandemonium** (`pandemonium`) before combat and exploration. Two additional
60-second edits give each opening chapter more room:
[Dementia](https://github.com/skulitom/LookingGlass/releases/download/v0.32.0-preview.1/LookingGlass-Chapter-1-Dementia.mp4) and [Pandemonium](https://github.com/skulitom/LookingGlass/releases/download/v0.32.0-preview.1/LookingGlass-Chapter-2-Pandemonium.mp4).

The arrival was captured from a fresh chapter start. The Blade, Gnome warning
and minecart scenes were replayed by starting at their actual pickup/trigger
locations in isolated sessions. The final footage uses the released game's
normal cinematic playback, not save-test fixtures. These are selected scene
replays, not evidence of an uninterrupted player route or full cinematic fidelity.
No generated gameplay frames, speed changes or replacement soundtrack were used.
Editorial titles, chapter labels, brief fades and a download card were added.
The existing three gameplay clips supply the later exploration/combat excerpts.

**All three videos are silent**, H.264, 1280 × 720, 30 fps. The recording session
has no audio output; in-game dialogue subtitles are enabled in the new scenes.
The captions leave the dialogue visible. Each final video was fully decoded,
checked for duration/format and reviewed at scene cuts before publication.
